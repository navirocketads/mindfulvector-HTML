Creatine is a naturally occurring compound found in your muscle cells and produced by your body in small amounts. It helps your muscles produce energy during high-intensity activity. Supplementing with creatine — particularly creatine monohydrate — is one of the most researched and well-supported strategies for improving strength, power output, and recovery.

Each serving (2 gummies) contains 3g of creatine monohydrate. We chose this dose because it's a clinically supported daily maintenance amount that's gentle on the stomach. Some athletes choose to take a larger 'loading' dose for the first week; our gummies allow for flexible dosing if your routine calls for it.

Most people notice improvements in strength, recovery, and training endurance within 2 to 4 weeks of consistent daily use. Creatine works through saturation of your muscle stores, so consistency matters more than timing.
