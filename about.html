Our Story

We're a small team of nutrition nerds and design obsessives who believe wellness should be easy to love.

🌱
Who we are

A different kind of supplement company.

MindfulVector was founded on a simple frustration: the supplement aisle is full of products that work, but almost no one enjoys taking them. Powders that clump. Capsules that smell like a vitamin closet. Gummies loaded with artificial junk.

We set out to fix it — starting with creatine, one of the most studied and beneficial supplements on earth. The result is a daily ritual that fits real life: clean, delicious, and effective.
