Cookie Policy

How and why we use cookies on mindfulvector.online.

Last updated: May 1, 2025

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and improve your experience. Similar technologies (such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage) work in similar ways and are covered by this policy.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential cookies

These are required for the website to function properly. They allow you to navigate the site, add items to your cart, and complete checkout. They cannot be disabled. Examples include session identifiers and cart-state storage.

Functional cookies

These remember your preferences (such as language or display settings) to make your experience more personalized.

Analytics cookies

These help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. They collect aggregated, anonymized information about pages viewed, time on site, and similar metrics. We may use providers such as Google Analytics for this purpose.

Marketing cookies

These may be used to deliver relevant advertising to you on other websites, measure ad campaign effectiveness, and prevent showing you the same ads repeatedly. We may use platforms such as Google Ads and Meta for this purpose.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our site are set by third parties we work with — including analytics, advertising, and payment processing providers. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies (note: some features of the site may not work)
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Set the browser to notify you when a cookie is being set

For more information on managing cookies, visit:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Opting Out of Analytics and Advertising

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

For interest-based advertising, you can use the opt-out tools provided by the Network Advertising Initiative (optout.networkadvertising.org) and the Digital Advertising Alliance (optout.aboutads.info).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about our use of cookies? Email privacy@mindfulvector.online.
